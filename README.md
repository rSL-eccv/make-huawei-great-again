# Make Huawei Great Again — Nova Phone Field Guide

A polished, responsive, fan-made showcase celebrating Huawei nova phones, with a special focus on the nova 9 SE and the requested nova 15 Max name.

## What's inside

- Editorial-style landing page with custom phone illustrations.
- Product highlights and a comparison of the two phone names.
- Detailed nova 9 SE information and expandable specification sections.
- Buyer checklist covering regional variants, app compatibility, charging and support.
- Source links and clear labels distinguishing confirmed information from unverified claims.

## Accuracy notes

This is an independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Huawei. The nova 9 SE specifications are general reference points and may vary by market. Check Huawei's local product page for the exact regional variant.

No official Huawei announcement or specification sheet for a "nova 15 Max" was verified while preparing this guide. Its specifications are deliberately marked **not confirmed** rather than guessed. Update that section only when reliable official information is available.

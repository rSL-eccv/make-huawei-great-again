02 / DEEP DIVE — DOCUMENTED MODEL

nova 9 SE.

✓Details vary by market. Check your local Huawei product page and device packaging.
108MP

HIGH-RESOLUTION MAIN CAMERA

The nova 9 SE pairs a 108 MP main camera with a 6.78-inch display and 66 W wired HUAWEI SuperCharge. Here’s the useful detail, without the fluff.

Huawei official specs ↗
01 Display −

6.78-inch LCD display, up to 90 Hz refresh rate, with a 2388 × 1080 resolution. A large canvas for video, reading and everyday scrolling.

02 Cameras +

Rear camera system: 108 MP main, 8 MP ultra-wide, 2 MP macro and 2 MP depth. Front camera: 16 MP. Camera modes and results can vary with software and conditions.

03 Performance & storage +

Qualcomm Snapdragon 680 4G platform. Memory and storage configurations differ by market; Huawei product pages list regional variants, so confirm the exact configuration before buying.

04 Battery & charging +

4000 mAh rated battery capacity and up to 66 W HUAWEI SuperCharge. Maximum charging speed requires a compatible charger and cable; actual speed varies by conditions. Charger availability may differ by region.

05 Build & connections +

Approx. 164.6 × 75.6 × 7.9 mm and 191 g. 4G LTE, USB-C and a side-mounted fingerprint reader. Network bands, SIM options and other connectivity features can vary by market.

06 Software & app services +

Originally shipped with Huawei EMUI software. App availability and services depend on region, software version and the apps you need. Before purchase, check that your essential apps, banking services, wearables and car integrations work on your exact device.

*Charging speed is a manufacturer-rated maximum, not a guarantee of real-world speed. Specifications are a general guide and may differ by region or production batch. Always use the local official product page as the final source.

05 / BUYER'S FIELD NOTES

Before you
make the move.

The best phone is the one that fits your apps, network, budget and day-to-day life. Use this checklist before buying any phone.

01 / VARIANT

Know your model

Phone specs can differ by country. Match the exact model code, RAM, storage, SIM configuration and supported network bands to the listing in your region.

02 / APPS

Check app essentials

Make a shortlist of must-have apps—especially banking, work, transit and authentication—and confirm they install and work on the exact software version.

03 / CHARGING

Read the small print

Fast charging depends on a compatible adapter, cable, temperature and battery state. Confirm what’s included in the box and use quality accessories.

04 / SUPPORT

Plan for ownership

Check warranty terms, repair options, parts availability, software support and return policy where you live—not just the headline price.
